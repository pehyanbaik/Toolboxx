About ToolBox

ToolBox is a collection of simple online utilities designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

What is ToolBox?

ToolBox brings together useful tools for working with images, text, QR codes and calculations in one place.

Our goal

The goal is simple: create useful tools with clear interfaces and as few unnecessary steps as possible.

Free to use

The tools currently available on ToolBox are designed to be free to use.
